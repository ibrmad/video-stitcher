# UI pass: one home per setting (compact plan)

> Run inline, as Modules 3–7 and 6b (owner: compact plan, no review
> pause; TDD, checks and a self-review stay). Branch
> `feat/desktop-ui-pass`, on top of `feat/desktop-m6b-ai` (not merged).

**Goal:** each setting lives in one place, and the controls say what they
are. Three areas, chosen and approved by the owner on 2026-10-06 after an
audit of every screen: Preferences, recording with the view bar, and
calibration tuning with its Save. (Lens, Stats and Help were left out;
the field outline waits for the auto-outline work.)

**Spec (as approved):**

1. **Preferences keeps app-wide settings only:** Recording (Codec,
   Folder) and Privacy (Usage data).
   - Gone: the export codec and quality defaults (the export sheet
     remembers its own choices: the same settings), recording quality
     (the view bar has it), the AI model (the export sheet has it), and
     the seam blend default.
   - The seam blend default is dropped, not moved: a second seam blend
     slider would duplicate Adjust's again. A recalibration keeps its
     blend (as before); a first calibration starts at 0.05. An old saved
     `default_blend` is ignored.
2. **View bar:** `Aspect [Auto ▾] … [● Record][▾]`.
   - Record starts; while recording the button reads the time with a
     stop square ("■ 0:12"), and a click stops.
   - ▾ opens a menu (Reco's own): Quality (Fast, Balanced, High, a ✓ on
     the current one), then "Codec and folder…" (opens Preferences). It
     is hidden while recording.
   - The separate quality dropdown and the recording badge go.
   - "Recording started" names the file, not its whole path.
3. **Calibration:** Save moves to the Adjust panel's title row, as
   `Adjust ……… Unsaved [Save]`, shown only while something is unsaved
   (Stitch, Lens or the field outline). ⌘S saves: File → Save
   Calibration in the macOS menu bar, listed in Keyboard shortcuts. Setup
   → Calibration keeps Recalibrate and Load file….
